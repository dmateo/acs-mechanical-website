About Biomass Boilers

Biomass boiler technology has been proven in Europe for over 30 years and is now established in North America. At ACS Mechanical Inc., we're proud to be a registered dealership for Hargassner Wood Chip and Pellet Boilers.

This technology provides a profitable solution to replace fossil fuel, lasts for years, and supports the growth of the local Yukon economy — all while leaving a smaller environmental footprint.

  • ✓High-efficiency renewable heating technology
  • ✓Automated fuel feeding & easy operation
  • ✓Reliable performance in cold climates
  • ✓Lower long-term heating costs
